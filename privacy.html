3. The LinienZauber app (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

LinienZauber needs no account of its own. We receive no personal data from the app. It contains no advertising, no analytics tools and no third-party data collection libraries. Without iCloud and Game Center, all data stays on your device.

Progress and settings. Your progress and settings such as volume and sound choice are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes them there.

iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also keeps your progress in your iCloud, which syncs it between your devices. It stays there after you delete the app. We have no access to it.

Game Center. Signing in to Game Center is optional. If you are signed in, the app sends your points and achievements to Apple. Other players can see them depending on your Game Center settings, for example on the leaderboards. Apple's privacy policy applies to this processing.

Motion sensor. On iPhone and iPad, tilting the device moves the background for the depth effect. The values are processed on the device only and are not stored. You can turn the effect off in the setup under “Parallax”.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or health data.

When you download the app via the App Store or TestFlight, Apple processes data under its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for that processing.
